Local-SEO glossary — use these terms correctly in generated copy:
- GBP = Google Business Profile (formerly GMB / Google My Business).
- NAP = Name, Address, Phone — consistency across directories is the core citations quality metric.
- Citation = any online mention of a business's NAP (directories, apps, map services).
- SERP = search engine results page; the "local pack" is the 3-result map unit at the top.
- Geo-grid = a map grid of ranking positions sampled at points around a location.
- SoLV = Share of Local Voice — % of grid points where the business ranks top 3.
- Review velocity = the rate of new reviews over time.
In UI copy, expand an acronym on first use ("Google Business Profile (GBP)"), then use the acronym.
