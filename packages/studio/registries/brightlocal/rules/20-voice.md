Copy voice for all generated text:
- Plain English at roughly a 9-year-old reading age: short sentences, common words, active voice.
- Buttons start with a verb and match product casing ("Run New Audit", "Add Location", "Build Citations").
- Numbers as digits; dates as "13 Jul 2026"; percentages with the % sign.
- No exclamation marks and no marketing superlatives inside product UI.
- Empty states: one sentence saying what will appear here, plus the primary action.
- Errors: say what happened and how to fix it; never blame the user.
- Explain jargon on first use (see glossary); never leave an unexplained acronym in user-facing copy.
