## What changed in the app tonight

Five streams, every fix reproduced live before it was touched and measured
after, which is why some of them turned out to be something other than what
the screenshot suggested:

- Showcase part two: the product's Animation controls, the real preset
  artwork, and Select Reviews rebuilt to their model.
- Get Reviews: the narrow template editor, the email preview's cut footer,
  the filtered campaigns count, the feedback preview arriving unanswered,
  and a DS finding (BreadcrumbList cannot truncate, so a five level trail on
  a phone can only spill; the answer is BreadcrumbEllipsis, not an override).
- Review Manager and Reply templates: the period menu, the reply-sent
  panel's numbers, the duplicated failure action, the templates empty state,
  the rule drawer's clipped card.
- Review Tracker and report settings: the Other tooltip, the date widow, the
  directory marks, the Trustpilot contradiction, the Send to list, the
  sidebar rule, and Last run against Last updated.
- Charts: a legend for the Source timeline, one shared x scale across the
  two Timeline charts, the squeezed last label, the donut running the same
  way as its legend, and the Facebook grouping surviving into the table.

Two reported defects were not defects. The hub's 836 five-star reviews is
right for this location; what was wrong was the words "ready to show", since
53 of them are Yelp and Showcase refuses to publish Yelp, so it reads 783
now. And the NPS line "running off the top of the chart" was never clipping:
nine campaign cards used a 300px scroll offset written for the Studio
screen's tall sticky header, so on the app the frame was a window onto the
card above.
