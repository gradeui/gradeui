## Morning brief (agreed late 10 Sep)

1. **Empty persona** (fifth, separate from trial): signed up, nothing
   connected, no campaigns, looking around. Own dataset with zero reviews.
   Every Reviews page gets a true empty state: a "Why it matters" stat band
   plus ONE action, in the product's own order: connect review sites
   (Google Business Profile first, it is the reply channel), ask for reviews
   (email, SMS, QR, drip campaigns), then reply and showcase.
2. **Trial persona keeps a half-connected account.** Its story is gating:
   what you unlock. New: a **"near the end of your free trial" modal** shown
   when you switch to the trial persona. "N days left, here is what the trial
   has done for you": reviews pulled in, replies sent, the campaign that
   spiked, the rating over the trial window, all from the rows. Then what
   stays and what stops when the trial ends, and the plan CTA in the site's
   pricing style (black numbers, green action). Gated features promoted as
   things already used, not things they might use.
3. **Reference pages in the notes.** Copy the matching website capture into
   each page's notes sheet with a one-line "what we borrowed".
4. **Terminology:** the site says "review sites" (and "80+ sites"), never
   sources, providers or directories (directories = citations). New copy
   uses "review sites"; existing "sources" strings stay until Ali has seen
   both side by side.
