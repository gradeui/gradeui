# Review Builder

Invite visitors to leave a review by email, SMS, kiosk or web link. The
campaigns table is the hub; Templates is a page of its own reached from
the header; New campaign is a wizard.

## URLs

The view is on the URL (`?view=templates`, `?view=wizard`), so Back
closes a view and a link can open one.

## Layout options

Three explorations sit beside the promoted page: a simplified builder,
a gauge-led Get Reviews concept, and a hub-and-spoke concept.


## Reference: the website page beside this one

The Reputation Manager page. What we borrowed: "Encourage reviews with branded emails, SMS or QR codes" and drip campaigns as the words for what the Builder does.
